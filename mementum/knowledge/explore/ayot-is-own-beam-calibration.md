# AYOT Is Own-Beam Calibration

> s313: Michael found arXiv 2608.01078v1 (`refs/2608.01078v1.pdf`,
> extracted to `.txt`) — Wang et al., Intel Labs China, "Attend to Your
> Own Thoughts: Breaking the Barrier for Post-Training Quantization of
> Reasoning LLMs through the Lens of 1.58-Bit Quantization"
> (ScaleQ-1.58 = CAT-Q ternarization + AYOT calibration). SECOND
> external paper the s308 theory explains on contact (after the owls
> paper) — and they named our clause in their title without knowing it.

## What the paper shows (their facts)

Ternary PTQ of Qwen3 models (1.7B→235B, dense + MoE), ~4M calibration
tokens. The entire finding is the calibration ablation (their Table 4,
Qwen3-4B W1.58A16, mean over Math-500/GSM8K/Omni-MATH/HumanEval+/MBPP+):

| calibration scheme | mean acc |
|---|---|
| generic text (C4/WikiText2) | ~0–3% — collapse |
| domain-specific Q/A pairs | 17.4% |
| CoT from a STRONGER model (DeepSeek-R1-671B) | 20.1% |
| **CoT self-generated by the target model (AYOT)** | **45.6%** |

Gains GROW as bits shrink (W4: scheme barely matters; W2/W1.58: the
whole game). Scales to 235B; 10⁶× fewer tokens than BitNet-from-scratch.
Code/models slated for release (BitTern repo).

## The headline: fourth triangulation of the own-state clause

The killer row: a 671B model's CoT — strictly better reasoning CONTENT —
is worth +2.6; the target model's OWN CoT is worth +25.5. **Content
quality is dominated by carrier match.** Bragg matching, measured a
fourth time, at a fourth scale:

1. **Inference** — s295 P-KV-1 (own-state KV channels)
2. **Recording geometry** — s304 (country key fires on NAME frames only)
3. **Training data** — owls paper (traits transfer same-base only)
4. **Quantization calibration** — AYOT (calibration transfers
   same-carrier only) ← NEW, external, industrial scale

One clause, four scales, two external labs unaware of each other's
result. λ triangulate at its strongest.

## Clause-by-clause mapping

- **L2 industrialized.** Ternarization is a RE-RECORDING; calibration
  context = the illumination under which fringe saliency is estimated.
  Generic text = wrong reference beam = reasoning fringes never lit =
  discarded = collapse. AYOT = "measure the beam before writing the
  plate" as a shipping method. Sharper than their own framing (web text
  lacks reasoning patterns): it is not the data CONTENT, it is the
  STATE DISTRIBUTION the model occupies during calibration.
- **Tape-resident reasoning, from the PTQ side.** The CoT must be IN
  CONTEXT because mid-reduction states exist only when the tape carries
  the trace; calibrating without it measures a model that never enters
  the states that matter.
- **Low-bit scaling = selectivity budget.** At W4 there is room to keep
  all fringes (beam moot); at 1.58 bits the budget forces choice, and
  choice requires the true beam. The fewer bits, the more quantization
  becomes ROUTING EXTRACTION — and routing extraction is beam-relative.
- **The residual gap = twin-image law.** With the right beam their
  ternary 4B reaches 58.4 Math-500 vs 96.8 fp16: AYOT picks the best
  exposures to preserve but cannot un-superpose an in-line plate
  (separability fixed at recording time; s306/s307 negatives). Contrast
  our regime: the DELTA ternarizes at retention 1.0 (off-axis). AYOT =
  best possible post-hoc compromise; the machine (M1/M5 born-quantized
  off-axis) remains the by-construction answer.

## Where the frame out-predicts theirs (discriminating tests, unfrozen)

Their explanation for own-CoT ≫ stronger-CoT is capability mimicry (the
ternary model cannot imitate a 671B). Ours is carrier mismatch.
Discriminable:

- **P-AYOT-PARAPHRASE** (P-SL-STRIP sibling): paraphrase the model's OWN
  CoT with another model (same content, foreign carrier) → carrier
  frame predicts degradation toward the stronger-LLM number;
  capability-mimicry predicts paraphrase harmless.
- **P-CRYSTAL-SURVIVAL** (bit-free quant metric, external validation
  target): run the crystal battery + type-gram stethoscope on
  AYOT-vs-C4 ternarized models (BitTern release) — predict the 9×9
  crystal (and type register) SURVIVES AYOT calibration and
  dies/degrades under C4, tracking the benchmark gap without running
  benchmarks. Zero-training validation of our instruments on someone
  else's artifacts.
