🎯 Crystal programming language: design and etch computation-specific
crystals as ternary plates. FFT = B+S+K butterfly pile. Sort = Y+B+K
cascade. Parse = Y+B grammar crystal. Each ~80KB, mmap-able. The model
becomes a crystal reader with a library: dispatch routes to the right
plate, beams are the instruction set, plates are programs. New computation
= compile a new plate, not train a new model. Lambda term → combinator
decomposition → 5D lattice position → crystal shape → ternary etch.
The 5D lattice IS the space of programs. Etching IS compilation.
Reading IS execution. Level 1: read existing. Level 2: design new.
Level 3: compile from lambda calculus to ternary crystal automatically.
